37. Notice (Native Dialog)

New

A lightweight modal alert built on the native <dialog> element. Supports Success, Error, Warning, and Information variants. Native modal behaviour provides keyboard dismissal and focus management. Backdrop-click dismissal is handled by the documentation JavaScript.

Payment Successful

Your transfer of SLE 500,000 has been sent to Aminata B.

Transaction Failed

Insufficient funds. Please top up your wallet and try again.

Low Balance

Your account balance is below SLE 10,000. Consider adding funds soon.

Information

Your account details have been updated successfully.

 
<button class="button green" onclick="document.getElementById('myNotice').showModal()">
  Open Notice
</button>

<dialog id="myNotice" class="notice success"
  aria-labelledby="myNoticeTitle"
  aria-describedby="myNoticeText">

  <button class="notice-close" aria-label="Close notice"
    onclick="this.closest('dialog').close()">
    <svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor"
      stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round">
      <path d="M18 6 6 18M6 6l12 12"/>
    </svg>
  </button>

  <div class="notice-icon">
    <svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor"
      stroke-width="2.5" stroke-linecap="round">
      <path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"/>
    </svg>
  </div>

  <h3 class="notice-title" id="myNoticeTitle">
    Payment Successful
  </h3>

  <p class="notice-text" id="myNoticeText">
    Your transfer has been sent.
  </p>

  <div class="notice-actions">
    <button class="button outline small"
      onclick="this.closest('dialog').close()">
      Cancel
    </button>
    <button class="button green small"
      onclick="this.closest('dialog').close()">
      Confirm
    </button>
  </div>
</dialog>
